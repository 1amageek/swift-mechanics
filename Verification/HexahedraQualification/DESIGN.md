## Contracts and Invariants
Six shared synchronous cases are affine patch/tangent, non-affine energy gradient, finite objectivity/rigid modes, mass/material assignments, geometry/binding refusals, and resources/cancellation. A seventh Native-only test awaits an actually cancelled Task. The shared caller executes the exact same six cases through a public protocol requirement. Assertions fail with typed fixture errors and retain original supplier error values.

Original natural ordering maps to cube nodes (000,100,110,010,001,101,111,011). Affine F=diag(1.1,1,1) gives E11=0.105, S=diag(1.689261,1.05,1.05), P=diag(1.8581871,1.05,1.05), energy 0.08844310125 J. Integral grad N_i=(s_i,t_i,u_i)/4 fixes every nodal force. The tangent oracle uses A[aI,bJ]=delta_ab S_IJ + lambda F_aI F_bJ + g(F_aJ F_bI+delta_IJ(FF^T)_ab)+2 beta(FE)_aI(FE)_bJ, with lambda=10, g=3.0441. Exact derivative shape integrals, not production Gauss data, fix all 576 entries. H=diag(0.2,-0.1,0.05) gives dP=diag(3.7238854,0.98618,2.05691) and energy rate 0.31913742 W. Original current moment, resultant and nodal virtual power are independently summed.

Cube consistent mass uses rho product_axis integral(N_i N_j), with each factor 1/3 for matching bits or 1/6 for differing bits. rho=6 gives row mass 0.75 kg. alpha=0.25 and v=(X,Y,Z) give consistent/lumped power 1.5/2.25 W. A disconnected second cell has twice K/G/beta/density and twice alpha, so force/energy/mass/damping scale 2/2/2/4, with no cross-cell entries. Sources, material assignments, boundary groups, node IDs and exact current state remain visible in results.

The warped reference map X=(x,y,z)+(0.2,0.1,0.1)xyz has physical Jacobian determinant 1+0.2yz+0.1xz+0.1xy and exact volume 1.1 m^3. Separable zero/first moment integrals fix every warped mass entry. Moving only node 6 by (-1.3,0,0) instead gives determinant 1-1.3yz: positive at every Gauss point but negative at a corner. Both reference and current whole-cube certificates must refuse it before any constitutive call. A stricter caller margin may conservatively refuse a valid cube; that is not classified as a proven inversion.

Analytic relative tolerance is 1e-8 (absolute floor 1); discrete central-difference step is 1e-6 and relative tolerance 2e-6. These values and oracles are frozen before execution. No failure weakens the oracle. Non-affine comparisons establish derivative consistency of selected quadrature, not exact continuum integration. Strain and volume domain errors remain original MaterialError values. Source binding failures remain exact HexahedralError values.
