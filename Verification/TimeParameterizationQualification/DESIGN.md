The selected domain is fixed-root spatial trees with fixed anchors and only fixed or scalar prismatic joints, static uniform gravity, held generalized applied forces, straight Euclidean waypoint segments and an explicit stop at every waypoint. This qualification does not establish general curved or rotating-robot retiming, global time optimality or formal interval arithmetic.

## Contracts and Invariants
The principal coupled fixture has world joint directions a1=(0,1,0), a2=(-0.8,0.6,0), moving masses 2, 3 and a fixed descendant mass 5. The first coordinate moves all three masses and the second moves the latter two. Translational kinetic energy independently yields M=[[10,4.8],[4.8,8]]. All rotations are fixed, so angular velocity and Coriolis bias vanish throughout the path. Uniform gravity (0,-10,0) projects to [-100,-48] N; held applied generalized forces [7,-2] N produce required static effort [93,50] N. COM offsets and positive asymmetric inertia remain explicit, although constant translational motion makes their rotational terms zero.

For a segment displacement d, independent derivative roots of f(u)=10u^3-15u^4+6u^5 give f'(1/2)=15/8 and f'' extrema +/-10/sqrt(3) at (3-/+sqrt(3))/6. Physical effort is M*d*f''/h^2+b. Each signed speed/acceleration/effort envelope is checked against independently derived extrema; endpoints include b. Duration lower bounds are the maximum of the minimum duration, signed speed quotients, and acceleration/effort square-root bounds. The declared floating-point safety factor and representable clock rounding may enlarge this lower bound; no stronger optimality is asserted.

Numeric comparison uses an explicit absolute/relative tolerance for independent SI values, with stricter metadata/endpoint/ledger equality where exact equality is contractual. Expected values are independent constants or closed-form expressions, never extracted from production certificates as their own oracle. Supplier replay supplements the independent oracle rather than replacing it. Typed error cases must match the original public RetimingError and nested supplier errors; any unexpected supplier failure fails qualification.

For this Native run, additional private cache, link artifacts and temporary allocation are limited to 128 MiB. Global free space must stay above 512 MiB plus a 64 MiB reaction reserve; the process group is stopped on budget, free-space or watchdog failure. Observations sample every half-second. This revised operational gate preserves the physical model, sources, oracles and tolerances. Root coordinates the two narrow execution slots; preparation performs no producer compilation. Every Swift Testing case has a one-minute time limit. Fixture construction and retiming failures are explicit typed failures, never fallback success. No unsafe pointer, error suppression or model/precision/oracle concession is introduced.
