# Constrained Sleep Evolution

## Purpose and Scope

This planned Hybrid child owns one directed root-located analytic geared-striker contact, genuine joint retained-row impulse consumption, connected sleep wake and atomic whole physical/history/event publication. Parent: [Hybrid](../DESIGN.md); no children. Serial prerequisites are StationaryIslandDynamics then IslandSleepContinuation and the qualified AF30 ConstrainedNormalImpulse lower. The selected source has resting sleeping connected gear A/B and independent moving striker C under the immutable direct-branch zero-external-load law. This is selected RB007/TI005..006 behavior, not full contact-stack, multiple coupled impact, friction/support, load or topology qualification.

IslandSleep owns original per-island physical/omission/awake acceleration and complete sleep/Integration records; this child consumes opaque issued endpoint/wake candidates. Existing Hybrid/EventEvolution/free impulse and old sleep paths remain frozen, including their generic unchanged-contributor rejection. No unconstrained solve, second velocity reconciliation, successful metadata-only wake or fake physical time is substituted.
