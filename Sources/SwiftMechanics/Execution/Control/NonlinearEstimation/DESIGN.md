Mean stays in physical SI. P and supplied interval Q are dimensionless normalized covariances: physical covariance is S*P*S. Input/checkpoint P must be symmetric positive definite, Q symmetric positive semidefinite, and physical encoder variance is finite positive m² with normalized R=variance/positionScale². Q is explicitly a discrete normalized perturbation covariance added once after the full declared interval; it is not silently interpreted as a continuous noise spectral density. Prediction is P-=F*P*F^T+Q. Position encoder observes q; normalized measurement is q/positionScale and exact H=[1,0]. Actual original encoder output must agree with mean/frame/unit/source/time before filter update.

Covariance arithmetic declares symmetry canonicalization: only differences within caller absolute/relative symmetry tolerance may be averaged; no diagonal jitter, eigenvalue clamp or absolute-value repair. Principal-minor PSD admission of Q preserves its values. Already equal off-diagonal entries are retained exactly; halving equal least-subnormal entries must not erase their nonzero coupling before admission. A zero diagonal requires the off-diagonal to be exactly zero before any scaled product; otherwise a nonzero squared off-diagonal may underflow and falsely admit an indefinite Q. In particular `[1,1e-200;1e-200,0]` is rejected as invalid covariance, without jitter, clamping or tolerance repair. Actual qualified Cholesky solves admit P and innovation covariance; original linear residual and finite outputs remain mandatory. Joseph P+=(I-KH)*P-*(I-KH)^T+K*R*K^T must pass symmetry and SPD again. Finite bounded innovation/NIS and posterior passive-domain checks can refuse an update. Position/rate observability is structurally admitted by the exact mechanical qdot=v and H=[1,0], whose continuous local observability rows [H;H*A] have nonzero rate coefficient rateScale/positionScale. Missing data does not fabricate a zero measurement or claim recovered information.
