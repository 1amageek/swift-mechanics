## Contracts and Invariants
For segment a = x[i+1]-x[i], l=|a| and reference l0, EA is the engineering axial rigidity. Bilateral spring-chain energy is EA/(2*l0)*(l-l0)^2, including compression. Tension-only cable energy is EA/(2*l0)*max(l-l0,0)^2: compression is slack with zero axial force and Hessian, extension is active. Its gradient exists at l=l0; its Hessian does not. Force-only evaluation and evolution admit that boundary; force-and-tangent evaluation returns `nonsmoothStretch` at exact equality, including at the rest state. No smoothing, numerical derivative or selected one-sided Hessian is substituted.

At interior node, u=a/|a| and v=b/|b| with b=x[i+1]-x[i]. With dual reference length h=(l0_prev+l0_next)/2, bending energy is EI/(2*h)*|u-v|^2 = EI/h*(1-u·v). This is the exact declared cosine turning model, with straight natural curvature; it is not an exact quadratic angle or torsional rod. Curved reference geometry is explicitly prestressed in bending: reference positions determine segment lengths and mass, not an intrinsic curvature tensor. All nondegenerate turns, including straight and antiparallel, are smooth; collapsed segments are refused. Zero second moment disables bending. Local analytic second-order chain-rule arithmetic differentiates these original energies; negative gradient is physical force, and returned stiffness is the positive energy Hessian, hence derivative of physical force is its negative. Ordered 3x3 block contributions are additive, not individually merged or assumed positive definite. Prestress may make stiffness indefinite.

Each reference segment mass is rho*A*l0, half to each endpoint. Every node therefore has positive lumped mass, with total mass rho*A*sum(l0). Drag is f_d=-alpha*m*v, C=alpha*M, power loss sum(alpha*m*|v|^2)>=0 in the declared stationary frame. Drag is an explicit environment momentum exchange, not an internally momentum-conserving material dashpot. Bending and stretch energies are invariant under superposed translation/rotation; their forces have zero net force and torque in exact arithmetic. Three translations and, for a stress-free straight chain, rigid rotations lie in stiffness nullspaces. There are no director, quaternion or twist DOF; torsional formulation is a marked typed refusal.

## Runtime Flows
Loads bind frame/revision/node order and hold explicit SI nodal force and gravity over the entire requested duration. A trial computes old physical internal, drag and external forces, advances free velocity then position, leaves fixed nodes unchanged, and reevaluates original potential. Acceptance checks original energy balance against external and drag work over actual displacement, nonpositive drag work (nonnegative work loss), total linear momentum against external/drag/support impulses, and total angular momentum about the frame origin against their old-position angular impulses. Those checks are separate from timestep iteration success. Failed physical acceptance halves the substep; minimum step, attempt count and numerical budget bound retries. Invalid input, cancellation, degenerate geometry or exhausted work rejects explicitly. A later failure discards all local accepted substeps. No partial state is published. There is no automatic tolerance relaxation, artificial stiffness or implicit/fallback model.

## State, Ownership, and Lifecycle
All public records and services are Sendable value types. Caller-owned NumericalWork records consumption even on rejection. Local arrays belong to one synchronous call and expire at its return. No global mutable state, shared cache, unsafe memory, task creation or synchronization branch exists. Inputs are retained immutably on rejection. Force-only output omits stiffness explicitly; it does not return successful zero stiffness. Material/source identity and curved-reference prestress remain visible in the returned model.

Arithmetic budgets charge conservative local upper bounds before each bounded analytic element, and storage reserves a conservative O(n) live-array envelope plus fixed local derivative temporaries.
