## Contracts and Invariants
Natural node order is (-,-,-), (+,-,-), (+,+,-), (-,+,-), (-,-,+), (+,-,+), (+,+,+), (-,+,+). N_i=(1+s_i ξ)(1+t_i η)(1+u_i ζ)/8. The reference map is X=ΣN_i X_i; J has physical coordinate rows and natural coordinate columns. Reference gradients are J_reference^-T times natural gradients; F=J_current J_reference^-1. Translation is removed before mapping arithmetic to limit cancellation. Admission checks det J over the entire natural cube conservatively: det J is tensor polynomial of degree at most two per axis. Its 27 values at {-1,0,1}³ are converted to tensor quadratic Bernstein coefficients; their lower bound must exceed the caller's positive determinant floor plus caller-selected numerical margin relative to the largest coefficient. Uncertified but possibly valid distorted cells fail explicitly; this is a sufficient certificate, not complete classification or an exact floating-point proof. Both reference and current mappings use the same certificate. Reference inverses also satisfy caller-selected Core relative tolerance. Material volume-ratio/strain limits apply at all eight constitutive points.

Full integration uses ±1/sqrt(3) with eight unit weights. E=Σ det(J0) psi(F); f_i=Σ det(J0) P grad_X N_i (positive energy gradient, N). K_ij e_a=Σ det(J0) dP[F; e_a⊗grad_X N_j] grad_X N_i (N/m). Supplier dP=h S+F dS includes geometric and material contributions exactly; no separate duplicated geometric term. K can be indefinite under prestress. The selected original Native fixture verifies stress-free rigid modes and finite-rotation covariance; this evidence does not qualify other profiles or constitutive histories.

Mass is M_ij=Σ rho det(J0) N_i N_j I (kg); row-sum lumping uses this same quadrature matrix. Total reference mass is Σ rho det(J0), volume in m³. C_ij=Σ alpha rho det(J0) N_i N_j I (kg/s), row-sum-lumped consistently with M, so f_d=Cv (N), dissipated power=vᵀCv (W). Power is evaluated through the positive quadrature quadratic form to avoid cancellation; it equals the matrix expression in exact arithmetic. Positive density and nonnegative alpha give positive mass and nonnegative damping in exact arithmetic. Three translations are node-major XYZ; all matrices are physical unscaled row-major 3N×3N values with explicit dimensions. The output retains the admitted immutable mesh, source, cell/material assignments, boundary groups and node order, plus the exact current state including its own source and revision. State mesh identity/revision/source/frame and all nodal identities must match; current provenance is declared by the caller and retained rather than inferred from mesh provenance.

Full-integration displacement Hex8 can exhibit volumetric locking near incompressibility and bending locking on coarse/distorted meshes. No reduced integration, hourglass stabilization, mixed pressure, selective integration or finite plastic history is silently substituted. Only the selected full-integration formulation enum exists; no callable placeholder for other families is introduced. Non-affine nonlinear energy and distorted-cell consistent mass are Gauss approximations; tangent exactly differentiates that discretized energy, not a claim of exact continuum integration.
