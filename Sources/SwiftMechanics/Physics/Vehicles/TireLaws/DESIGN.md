## Contracts and Invariants
SI units: m, s, rad, m/s, rad/s, N, N*m and W. A contact-frame quaternion maps tire x-forward/y-axle/z-road-normal to the common reference frame. The wheel is uncambered and has angular velocity omega*y only; the road translates without angular velocity. This reduced motion domain is encoded by a scalar spin input. Radius R is the supplied calibrated loaded/effective rolling radius and the center-to-contact distance in this selected rigid geometry. Contact position is center-R*z, and must lie on the supplied plane within the caller's dimensional geometry tolerance. Relative normal center/road speed must satisfy the caller's normal-speed tolerance. Normal load is a positive supplied scalar; this law returns tangential loads only and never adds the normal owner's force or work.

For v = centerVelocity-roadContactVelocity, vx=x dot v, vy=y dot v, u=abs(vx), kappa=(R*omega-vx)/u and alpha=atan2(vy,u). The road-frame contact slip is (vx-R*omega,vy). Reverse rolling uses the same right-handed spin convention. Positive omega produces surface velocity -R*omega*x. Evaluation requires u at least the calibration's strictly positive minimum: standstill and low-speed transitions explicitly fail, with no epsilon denominator or automatic alternate model.

This implementation is an explicitly defined **radial combined-brush approximation**, not a Pacejka or Fiala implementation. Its formulation revision is `radial-brush-v1`: q=(Ck*kappa,-Ca*vy/u), Q=norm(q), L=mu*Fz. If Q=0, F=(0,0); otherwise a=min(Q/L,3), f=L*a*(1-a/3+a*a/27), and F=f*q/Q. For Q>=3L, f=L exactly. Both stiffnesses are positive N per dimensionless slip/tangent; mu>0 is calibrated for the selected road/tire pair. The shared capacity enforces norm(F)<=L. The polynomial has unit slope at zero and continuous value/first derivative at saturation. Positive stiffness and a common nonnegative radial multiplier enforce F dot slip<=0 even with unequal longitudinal/lateral stiffness.

Primary reference: [Project Chrono 9.0.1 ChFialaTire.cpp, commit 2617649bf687456328a122b63dc0bb64df91394a](https://github.com/projectchrono/chrono/blob/2617649bf687456328a122b63dc0bb64df91394a/src/chrono_vehicle/wheeled_vehicle/tire/ChFialaTire.cpp#L130-L185), consulted 2026-10-05. Its lines 130–132 establish slip conventions, and 177–185 give a cubic lateral brush saturation. Our radial combination and common cone are a separately stated approximation: Chrono computes separate longitudinal/lateral branches and also an aligning moment. Those branches and moment are not represented here. Calibration must explicitly identify this formulation, rather than reusing another model's coefficients without a fit.

The selected rolling model is an explicitly calibrated Coulomb moment, Mr=-ell*Fz*sign(omega), ell>=0 in m; Mr=0 at omega=0. The sign jump is deliberate and reported through the spin input, without smoothing or a static-friction claim. It is not a bristle state. No aligning moment is defined. The returned center torque is (-R*z) cross F + Mr*y; the road contact reaction has force -F and pure couple -Mr*y. The road body's translating-only motion makes its couple power zero.

Physical wheel power is returnedCenterWrench dot (omega*y,centerVelocity); road power is returnedRoadForce dot roadContactVelocity. Slip dissipation is -F dot slip, rolling dissipation is -Mr*omega. Acceptance evaluates the returned world-frame force/torque, not only the constitutive scalar formula: both losses must be nonnegative and wheel+road power nonpositive within explicit W tolerance, their sum must balance wheel+road power, and force norm must satisfy the original shared cone within explicit N tolerance. Passivity tolerance scales only with losses/reference power; balance tolerance also accounts for the separate wheel/road powers. A common translational boost cannot silently loosen the passivity test. Negative losses within rounding tolerance remain reported, never clamped. A law with missing energy history does not report stored energy or integrated work.

## Failure, Concurrency, and Constraints
Typed failures separate invalid input/calibration/policy, stale calibration/reference, frame mismatch, outside calibrated envelope, low-speed domain, geometry mismatch, nonfinite arithmetic, physical acceptance, and supplier Core/Load errors. Cancellation is checked through LoadWork before metadata bytes, before arithmetic and before return. Work cost: one unit per metadata UTF8 byte (all keys participating in equality plus source/formulation), then one fixed law evaluation. Fixed finite scalar arithmetic/validation is included in that evaluation, not a wall-clock promise. Reserve 192 scalar slots including fixed input/result/intermediate values; no component-owned numerical arrays. Metadata is borrowed and separately charged; retained results in a composition require caller aggregate budgeting. Arithmetic overflow/underflow that removes positive force capacity or nonzero slip demand fails explicitly. No retries or partial response.
