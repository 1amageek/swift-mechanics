## Contracts and Invariants
Each dimension >=3; checked N=nx*ny*nz precedes traversal/allocation. Flatten (k*ny+j)*nx+i. Unique periodic faces u(i,j+1/2,k+1/2), v(i+1/2,j,k+1/2), w(i+1/2,j+1/2,k); pressure at cell centers. D uses forward differences, G uses backward differences, so G=-D-transpose and -DG is positive on mean-free fields. Solve -DG p=-(rho/dt)D(v*) without a dense matrix. CG uses the analytic connected periodic-grid SPD certificate on the mean-free subspace, retains original RHS, projects search/residual drift to mean-free and recomputes the original full residual before returning. Gauge subtracts arithmetic mean; its remaining mean must meet caller pressureGaugeAbsolute [Pa]. No reported solver convergence bypasses full-cell physical residuals [Pa/m²] or corrected divergence [1/s].

Predictor R=-div(a*donor(v))+nu*lap(v)+source with nu=mu/rho. Each cross-component advecting face is the average of the two staggered neighbor velocities straddling its dual face. Matching component direction uses adjacent averages. Right/top/front flux chooses donor by advecting sign, and left/bottom/back uses the same shared flux. Update v*=v+dt R; correction vNew=v*-(dt/rho)G p. Step entry requires original divergence tolerance, duration/envelopes and checked time/sequence advance. Project-only leaves time/sequence/source unchanged. A step advances once after all physical checks. State pressure may carry any finite constant offset; every produced pressure has the admitted mean gauge.

Every dual row requires dt*sum(outgoing advecting rates/h)+2nu*dt*sum(h^-2)<=caller courantLimit<=1. The measured maximum and separate donor/viscous factors are returned. This is the explicit discrete domain, not unconditional Navier-Stokes stability.

Cell mass m=rho*dx*dy*dz. Periodic mass remains rho*Lx*Ly*Lz. All three global momenta change only by m*N*dt*source. K=0.5*m*sum(v²). Viscous power=m*nu*sum(direction edge delta(v)²/h²); donor loss=0.5*m*sum(|aFace|*delta(v)²/h); divergence transport power=-0.5*m*sum(v²*dualDivergence). Old-velocity source power=m*sum(v*source); explicit injection=0.5*m*dt²*sum(R²). Independently check advective and viscous dot-work, predictor energy, correction force, full-step momentum and energy. Projection loss=0.5*m*sum(delta(v)²); pressure residual work=dt*m/rho*sum(p*D(vNew)). Knew-Kpred+loss-pressureWork must vanish within caller energy tolerances. Unforced unexplained energy growth is rejected with measured divergence/pressure leakage allowance.

## Runtime Flows
Call-local bounded buffers only; no per-cell array materialization. Whole component fields use immutable COW borrows for traversal, with owned predictor/output buffers. Every loop polls caller cancellation and Task cancellation. Scalar ceilings include simultaneously live input/output/rate/RHS/pressure-supplier storage; checked work is charged before arithmetic and checked storage before allocations. The pressure supplier polls before initial buffer materialization, charges a checked4*N initialization/copy upper bound (three zero-filled vectors and the residual COW copy first materialized by mean removal), charges4 per original RHS finite/max-norm admission, and charges1 per polled direction assignment. Both initial and exact-zero-residual restart direction copies follow that same contract. A final cancellation poll precedes successful pressure publication. For a zero RHS the complete supplier cost is65*N+2 operations, zero iterations and6*N peak scalar storage; the complete projection cost is374*N+45 operations and20*N peak storage. These are sums of the explicit original primitive charges, not fitted measurements. Successful pressure supplier work is absorbed from its returned ledger. A thrown supplier has unknown work, explicitly marked failedSupplierWorkUnavailable; computation stops. Caller-side numerical resource failures retain the known caller ledger.
