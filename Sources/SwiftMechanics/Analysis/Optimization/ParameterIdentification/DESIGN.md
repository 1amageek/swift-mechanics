# Physical affine mass and damping identification

## Purpose and Scope
Parent: [Optimization](../DESIGN.md). Children: none. Own AF30 / the selected OP-009 physical identification operation: positive mass and passive linear dashpot coefficient of one fixed-base spatial prismatic body from force observations with supplied exact kinematics. Design is fixed before Swift declarations; implementation and behavioral/profile qualification are pending. Full OP-009 responsibility remains assigned beyond this explicit domain.

## Responsibilities and Boundaries
Consume real tree/dynamics/load/derivative operations, solve the resulting bounded two-parameter convex problem and independently accept the original physical residual objective and parameter KKT. Publish parameter bounds, physical estimates, residuals, objective, numerical identifiability directions and explicitly scoped uncertainty assumptions. Neither a supplied regression matrix nor a scalar finite-difference wrapper is the production mechanical model.

Geometry, input trajectory reconstruction, observation acquisition and runtime evolution are caller authorities. The operation admits a fixed root, one prismatic child, static anchors, fixed axis and orientation, fixed body COM and a known inertia shape per unit mass. Candidate mass scales the selected body's mass and COM inertia together; known root inertia is unchanged. The only unknown load is a translational linear dashpot, evaluated through ScalarLoadEvaluating with zero spring and cubic terms. Observed applied conjugate force is the sole excitation. Gravity, additional loads, flexible/contact mechanics, unknown inertia shape/COM/geometry, latent states and nonlinear parameter mappings are outside this selected model. Unsupported supplied mechanics must fail before sampling; callable admission guards carry the required incomplete-domain marker. No bearing-reaction or full inertia-identification claim follows from translation data.

## Architecture
```text
immutable prismatic source + exact q/v/a observations + measured force/sigma
 -> source/layout/unit/capacity admission
 -> real dashpot + tree/dynamics + original Newton-Euler residual at reference parameters
 -> exact mechanical mass/damping directions -> weighted physical design matrix
 -> rank/direction proof -> bounded SPD QP via OptimizationSolving
 -> fitted mass/dashpot + ORIGINAL Newton-Euler residuals and directions
 -> original objective/box KKT + information inverse residual
 -> immutable estimate or typed phase-specific failure
```

## Contracts and Invariants
The required service is `PhysicalParameterIdentifying.estimate(_:policy:workspace:loadWork:supplierWork:work:) throws(ParameterIdentificationFailure) -> PhysicalParameterEstimate`. The problem owns an immutable `PrismaticIdentificationSource`, ordered `ForceObservation` records, two finite physical parameter bounds and caller SI parameter reference scales. Source binds the actual tree/body/frame/joint/model revision, reference inertias and dashpot stroke/rate envelope. Observation fields are an immutable KinematicState, appliedForceNewtons and forceStandardDeviationNewtons. Kinematic time/revision/chart/layout, acceleration shape and all finite values are checked against the captured source; acceleration is supplied as observed generalized acceleration, never reconstructed from finite differences. Each sigma must be finite and strictly positive. Parameter identity order is mass then damping; IDs must be distinct. Mass has units kg; damping has units N s/m=kg/s. Translation position/rate/acceleration use m, m/s, m/s². Force observations are the signed effort conjugate to the same prismatic coordinate, in N.

Candidate inertia uses the fixed reference COM and Ic(m)=m*IcReference/mReference. Actual Model mass-property validation admits each constructed physical value and the derivative supplier's caller-selected neighborhood. The real ScalarLoadEvaluator evaluates Qd=-d*v, potential zero and dissipatedPower=d*v² under its declared finite envelope. The fixed physical source makes the observation residual exactly affine in m,d. At each sample the residual is defined by originalInertialForce(includeBias:true) minus assembled applied load and measured excitation. Exact MechanicalDifferentiating directions provide dM*a+dBias-dForce. The admitted unit-axis translation provides an independent analytic cross-check of returned original inertial force and parameter columns; these checks reject wrong physical suppliers and do not replace their operations or values. The damping direction explicitly supplies dQd=-v as the analytic parameter partial of this admitted constitutive law; it is not confused with the load provider's velocity partial. No custom force callback, nonlinear residual-as-optimality or inferred mechanical Hessian is required by this domain.

Normalize physical parameters by their positive SI references and each force residual by its supplied sigma. The objective is dimensionless `0.5*sum((R_i/sigma_i)^2)`. Bounds are transformed by the same parameter references; scaling changes conditioning, not parameters or physical residuals. Use the real derivative columns and reference residual to construct the affine objective, not an independently substituted scalar regression equation. At the fitted parameters reconstruct the actual inertia/dashpot, recompute ORIGINAL Newton-Euler residuals and exact directions, and independently verify affine agreement, objective, gradient, box feasibility, multiplier sign, stationarity and complementarity. Noisy residuals need not be zero: publication certifies their objective and KKT, not that noisy observations are equilibrium solutions. A supplied optimizer's certificate is evidence to check, not authority to skip physical acceptance.

Identifiability is computed from the original weighted, parameter-normalized mechanical design matrix before optimization. Caller-controlled numerical rank screening reports full rank or a typed unidentifiable result with identifiable/null directions and independently checked matrix-times-null residual. Exact zero rank loss and a positive pivot below caller threshold are distinguished; the latter is rank-indeterminate. Box restrictions do not convert correlated data into physical identifiability. SPD QP and information-inverse Cholesky follow only after full-rank admission. No rank or condition assertion is inferred from a low objective.

The noise model is explicitly independent, zero-mean Gaussian force noise with known per-observation sigma and exact supplied kinematics/model. Publish the normalized information matrix and its inverse transformed to physical parameter coordinates as `unconstrainedGaussianReferenceCovariance`. This is the exact covariance of the unbounded affine Gaussian estimator under that stated model; it is not the sampling covariance or confidence region of the bounded estimator, including at a realized interior optimum. Publish active bounds alongside this distinction. Unknown sigma, kinematic uncertainty, correlated measurement noise, confidence intervals and latent-state uncertainty are not inferred. The information inverse is accepted by an independent original matrix-product residual. Without these assumptions the corresponding observation model is refused rather than fabricating uncertainty.

## Runtime Flows
Admission precedes allocation and metadata comparisons. Evaluate observations with reusable buffers, construct and rank-screen the physical design, invoke the bounded optimizer once, and independently evaluate fitted mechanics before publication. Information solves and all numerical phases use remaining budgets with reserved live storage. A failed supplier stops the operation once; there is no retry, alternate precision/model or least-squares fallback after rank refusal. Caller cancellation is checked during owned traversal, before/after each supplier block and immediately before publication. Nested synchronous suppliers retain their own admitted bounded block and Task-cancellation semantics.

## State, Ownership, and Lifecycle
Source, observation, problem, policy and output records are immutable Sendable. `IdentificationWorkspace` is caller-owned exclusive inout storage for reusable design/residual/rank/info/output buffers. MechanicalDerivativeWorkspace and EnumerationWorkspace have separate phase-local owners: reuse each within its phase, release it before the next phase. No private supplier storage inspection is needed; exposed supplier peak logical storage conservatively reserves retained result/workspace slots while an original physical query is nested. Inputs are borrowed immutable owners; source arrays are materialized only at the declared supplier input boundary and publication, with their live scalar/copy slots precharged. No inner row loop creates row arrays. No accepted mechanics state is modified. There is no shared cache, unsafe view, target-conditioned storage/conformance, persistence or RNG. The owner workspace retains initialized capacity between calls until its caller releases it; supplier workspaces have the shorter phase lifetime stated above.

## Failure, Concurrency, and Constraints
Caller policy fixes maximum observations, metadata bytes, logical live slots/fill, rank and physical/certificate/information tolerances, mass/damping bounds/reference quantities, law envelope, derivative physical neighborhood, explicit Float64/referenceCPU linear capabilities and cancellation. NumericalWork counts owned arithmetic/initialization/copies and invocation units; LoadWork retains actual ScalarLoadEvaluator logical work; DerivativeSupplierWork retains actual derivative invocation attempts. Opaque fixed-dimension Core/Model validation calls are counted separately as supplier calls, without relabeling their internal arithmetic as known numerical operations. Checked products/sums precede allocations; no guessed iteration/candidate cap replaces caller policy.

Each mutable inout supplier invocation uses a captured, primed remaining-budget ledger. Scalar load units include one driver attempt plus actual supplier units; derivative invocation attempts are also explicitly primed in their existing call ledger. LinearSolving instead receives its required immutable remaining budget; its returned work must match that budget and is absorbed before output checks. Budget/counter replacement or reset is refused, including after throw; restore the known prefix, retain underlying cause and explicitly mark unavailable supplier consumption. Successful exposed work is absorbed once before output validation. Failed derivative/dynamics/optimizer work retains its actual known inout prefix and underlying typed error; producer unavailable-work flags propagate conservatively. Opaque failed LinearSolving has unavailable consumed work and terminates without continuation. Original source/metadata, array dimensions and finite outputs are rechecked after invocation. Failures retain phase, known work, supplier attempts, last original residual/objective if available and unavailable-work metadata. Invalid observation/source/unit/noise model, unidentifiable/indeterminate rank, nonfinite, rejected original evidence, unsupported domain, budget and cancellation never return an estimate.

## Verification and Change Impact
The dedicated test contract owns independent analytic mass/damping/weighted-noise, correlation/null-direction, SI scaling, rotation, bound/KKT, original-recomputation and failed supplier/work evidence. Actual forward dynamics generates synthetic kinematic accelerations; original Newton-Euler and passive load calls are exercised by estimation. Finite differences are optional independent directional oracles only. Production cannot accept a manufactured design matrix. Changes to physical source parameterization, unit/observation association or supplier work authority require rechecking this child and its estimator consumers. No qualification is claimed from this design or future build alone.
