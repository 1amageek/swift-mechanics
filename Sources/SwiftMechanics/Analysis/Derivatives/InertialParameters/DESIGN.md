## Responsibilities and Boundaries
Publish ten body-frame SI coordinates in the fixed order `(m,hx,hy,hz,Ioxx,Ioyy,Iozz,Ioxy,Ioxz,Ioyz)`. A binding is created from an actual `BodyRecord3D` inertial representation and captures its source provenance, body/frame instance identities, model revision, reference pose and physical properties. Ordered unique parameter IDs identify scalar coordinates; repeated CAD occurrences use distinct body IDs and distinct scalar IDs. Callers retain source revision authority and explicitly declare whether their parameter mapping preserves topology. This component checks the supplied current source against the captured representation; it cannot query an external CAD database. Signed direction coefficients are tangent values, not physical masses or inertias.

## Contracts and Invariants
`h=m*c`, `Io=Ic+m*A(c)`, `A(c)=(c dot c)Identity-c*c^T`. Directions obey `dc=(dh-c*dm)/m`, `dIc=dIo-dm*A(c)-m*dA(c)`, with `dA(c)=2(c dot dc)Identity-dc*c^T-c*dc^T`. Tensor symmetry is exact in this chart; six tensor coordinates each represent one symmetric component, without an off-diagonal factor of two.

For caller positive neighborhood radius r, both actual linear parameter endpoints `(m,h,Io)+/-r*(dm,dh,dIo)` are converted to physical properties and admitted by the published Model constructor with physicalityRelative=0. Positive mass and PSD augmented second moment are a convex parameter domain; endpoint admission therefore covers the interval, subject to finite arithmetic. The existing mechanical supplier additionally requires its own straight COM/central-inertia tangent endpoints; this deliberate intersection may reject a physically valid parameter segment and never enlarges the supplier domain. Endpoint evaluation is admission only, never a finite difference derivative.

Tree revision, binding revision, direction revision, body/frame/order, source provenance, reference pose and inertial properties must agree. All parameter IDs are unique across occurrences. Gravity is absent or uniform in the world frame; its value, time and frame remain fixed. Supplied body/generalized force contributions are explicitly declared independent of these parameters. Custom force callbacks are refused before invocation because this product does not publish their inertial-parameter derivative contract. `dRequiredDrive=dM*a+dBias-dQ`. Independent body force/torque differentiation at the actual fixed state supplies the acceptance residual after the existing `S_i/E` scaling. Original primal inverse residual, actual energy, mechanical primal agreement and forward implicit residual are preserved.

## Failure, Concurrency, and Constraints
Typed failures distinguish stale mapping, topology change, unsupported force derivative, invalid direction/chart, supplier errors, numerical exhaustion, cancellation and original differentiated residual rejection. Suppliers are invoked once per operation with remaining budgets and reserved storage; failed supplier consumption flags are retained by the embedded `DerivativeError`/`DynamicsError`. Extra live storage uses a conservative bound `200*B+40*N+2*N*N+10*W+N*F+1024`. The actual cumulative supplier peak is conservatively reserved as live storage for later inverse/energy calls, including all tangent and local workspace records; this may refuse a tighter budget without weakening accounting. Integer products/sums are checked. Source strings and body/frame keys are scanned and charged before bounded equality. Local arithmetic and comparison work is charged before execution; supplier invocation attempts are charged separately. Physical constructors are counted as public supplier attempts. No hidden backend, FD, fallback or synthesized primal result is admitted.
